2. NASA / USGS VIKING MARS MOSAIC

Mars Viking Colorized Global Mosaic (MDIM 2.1). Credit: NASA Ames / NASA JPL / USGS Astrogeology Science Center.
https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_viking_colorized_global_mosaic_232m
USGS Astrogeology's metadata describes the mosaic as public domain with no use constraints:
https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=1efb16809db84f0c892b9b0662dab0c8
The game's embedded JPEG has the same 1024 x 512 dimensions and near-identical pixels as the USGS 1024-pixel downloadable image, but its bytes differ, consistent with re-encoding.
